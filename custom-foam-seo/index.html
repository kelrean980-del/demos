FAQ

Questions people ask before ordering foam

How do I measure foam for a sofa cushion?

Measure the cushion cover (length, width and thickness) and add half an inch for a snug fit. Our guide shows it step by step.

What foam is best for boat seats?

Open-cell dry-fast foam drains water and resists mildew, so it is the usual choice for marine and outdoor cushions.

Can you cut foam from a template?

Yes. Send a photo or a paper template with measurements and we cut the exact shape.

Do you ship to my state?

We ship custom foam to all 50 states. Delivery time depends on your ZIP code.
